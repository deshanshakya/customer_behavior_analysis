Customer Behavior Analysis 
A data-driven project aimed at decoding retail shopping patterns to help management optimize marketing, improve customer satisfaction, and drive long-term loyalty.

Objective
Analyze a comprehensive consumer behavior dataset to answer a core business question: How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

Tech Stack & Architecture
Data Processing & Exploration: Python, Pandas, Jupyter Notebook

Database & Querying: PostgreSQL (Data ingestion, relational modeling, and behavioral SQL queries)

Data Visualization (Upcoming): PowerBI

 Key Focus Areas
Demographics & Channels: Analyzing shifting purchasing patterns across online vs. offline sales channels and different demographic segments.

Conversion Drivers: Evaluating the impact of discounts, product reviews, seasonal shifts, and payment preferences on repeat purchases and consumer decisions.
